1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Factictionary ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you install and play Tambola Live: Housie Bingo 90 ("the App"). We are firmly committed to user privacy, transparent data practices, and full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Pure Virtual Entertainment: Tambola Live is designed solely for family amusement and casual social entertainment. All coins, points, and prizes within the game are 100% virtual tokens with zero cash value. We do NOT offer real-money gambling, physical prizes, or real-cash withdrawals.

2. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous User ID & Device Identifiers: We utilize Firebase Authentication to generate an anonymous, encrypted unique user identifier (UID) for each installation. This allows you to host rooms, join multiplayer games, and store your game points without requiring personal phone numbers or passwords.
  • Player Display Name: An optional handle chosen by you (e.g., "LuckyPlayer") displayed to other players in multiplayer game lobbies.
  • Game Data & Progression: Virtual points balance, hosted rooms, active ticket states, and game completion records stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Purchases & Billing Tokens: When you purchase coin packs, VIP passes, or subscriptions, financial transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing. We never receive, store, or process your credit card, debit card, or bank account details.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs to provide reliable cloud synchronization, advertising, and billing services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For real-time multiplayer room sync, leaderboard scores, and cloud backup.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to deliver optional rewarded video ads (providing free coin refills when requested by the player) and native banner ads. AdMob may collect coarse device identifiers and diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: For secure processing of digital in-app products and subscriptions.

4. Local Device Storage & Caching

To ensure high performance and minimize internet data usage, the App caches certain gameplay data locally in device storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Randomized 1–90 number call sequence for the active game session (ensuring game calls continue seamlessly even during momentary network fluctuations).
  • Your latest cached points balance and custom ticket theme preference.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is a family-friendly casual board game suitable for all ages. However, because the game utilizes simulated casino-style board game mechanics and in-app purchases, we comply with Google Play Families Policies. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Data Security & Retention

All communications between the App and our backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User progression and room logs are maintained only as long as necessary to provide game functionality.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated game data at any time. To request account deletion, navigate to the in-app settings screen or contact our support team at factictionary@gmail.com. All records associated with your UID will be permanently removed within 30 days.
